Modern Gait Recognition: A Survey from Deep Learning to Foundation Models

Chao Fan, Dingqiang Ye, Kewei Ou, Shiqi Yu, Vishal M. Patel, Jianqiang Li, and Chengwen Luo

Abstract

Gait recognition identifies individuals from body shape and motion during walking. This survey reviews advances during 2020–2026 across five dimensions: modalities, backbone architectures, learning frameworks, real-world noise handling, and metric design. It connects task-specific deep learning with foundation-model adaptation, gait-specific pretraining, and synthetic data. Published results from seven major benchmarks and selected cross-domain evaluations are analyzed alongside inputs, training resources, and protocols to identify what works, where it fails, and what transfers. A progress-and-limitations synthesis shows comparatively mature controlled cross-view recognition, persistent clothing and domain sensitivity, and conditional benefits from complementary inputs and pretraining. Dedicated discussions examine language guidance, identity preservation and real-data benefits of synthesis, and scaling with data volume, identity diversity, condition coverage, model size, and computation. The outlook addresses whole-body recognition, healthcare, agents, and privacy. An open companion website provides source-linked papers, benchmark results, and dataset and modality statistics: https://chaofan996.github.io/gait-survey.
